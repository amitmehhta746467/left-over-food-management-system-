ABOUT LEFTOVERBITE

Good food belongs
on someone's table.

LeftoverBite helps people with surplus food connect with local people and organizations who can use it. We make it easier to share food before it goes to waste.

Share surplus food → Find available food

OUR MISSION

Make sharing simple.

We bring donors and local food recipients together around practical, timely food rescue. Every listing is an opportunity to make a useful connection in the community.

🤝
COMMUNITY FIRST

Local food, local impact.

Nearby listings help food find a recipient without adding complicated steps. Donors and requesters coordinate pickup details around what works for them.

🕒
SAFETY MATTERS

Clear details help.

Donors can describe the food, servings and safe pickup window so recipients can make informed requests and plan collection.

QUICK ANSWERS

Good to know

Who can post a donation?

Restaurants, shops, community groups, event organizers and households with surplus food can post a listing.

How do I find food near me?

Open Find Food and search by city or area. You can also filter listings by food type.

How is pickup arranged?

Recipients can submit a request with a pickup note. Please coordinate the exact time and handoff details directly and follow applicable food-safety guidance.
